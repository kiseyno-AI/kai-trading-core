# KAI Core glossary

- **Daily Review** examines the evidence from one closed KRX session and produces separate feedback for the signal and short-term deciders.
- **WAIT observation** records a tracked WAIT decision and any observed trade-price or best-ask event. A confirmed execution is a separate outcome.
- **Candidate contract** is a versioned draft of one decider's operating instructions, derived from review feedback for human review.
- **Weekly learning artifact** is a separately validated bundle of historical evidence eligible for future decider input.
